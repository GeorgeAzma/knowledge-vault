Amount of electrons flowing per second, like velocity of ball rolling down a hill
### Formula $I=\dfrac{V}{R}=\dfrac{Q}{t}$
- $V$ [[voltage]]
- $R$ [[resistance]]
- $Q$ [[charge]]
> [!example] 
> - Steeper the hill (high $V$) faster the ball rolls (high $I$)
>   bumpier the hill (high $R$) slower the ball rolls
> - More pressure in pipe faster the water flows
>   Tighter the pipe slower the water flows 
>   `assuming same pressure or V`
### Unit Amp $A=C/s$
- $C$ [[Charge|coloumb]]
### Ampere's Law $B=\dfrac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}$
- $\mu_0$ [[magnetic constant]]
- $r$ distance from wire
