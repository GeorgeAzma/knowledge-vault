[[Physics/Electricity/Power|Power]] radiated by an accelerating electric [[charge]] `assuming acceleration is much less than speed of light`
### $P=\dfrac{q^2a}{6\pi\epsilon_0c^3}$
- $P$ radiated power `W`
- $q$ [[charge]] `C`
- $a$ acceleration `m/s^2`
- $\epsilon_0$ [[vacuum permittivity]]
- $c$ [[Light#Speed of Light $c=f lambda=299,792,458m/s$|speed of light]]
so any accelerating electric [[charge]] emits electromagnetic radiation
