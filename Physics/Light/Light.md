### Speed of Light $c=f\lambda=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}}=299,792,458m/s$
- $f$ wave frequency
- $\mu_0$ [[magnetic constant]]
- $\epsilon_0$ [[vacuum permittivity]]
### Photon Energy $E=hf=hc/λ$
- $h$ [[Planck's Constant]]
- $\lambda$ Wavelength
