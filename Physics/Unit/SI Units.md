### Base Units
1. $s$ [[second]]
2. $m$ [[meter]]
3. $kg$ kilogram
4. $A$ [[Current|ampere]]
5. $K$ [[kelvin]]
6. $\text{mol}$ [[mole]]
7. $\text{cd}$ [[candela]]
##### Derived
- $m=c\cdot s$ just seconds multiplied by the constant speed of light
- $\text{mol}=6.02214076\times 10^{23}$ counting unit `like dozen`
- $K=\dfrac{E}{k_B}$ just average [[kinetic energy]] of a system, divided by [[boltzmann constant]]
    - $E$ in [[joule]] $J=kg~m^2s^{-2}$
- $\text{cd}$ just radiant intensity $W/\text{sr}$ weighted by human eye sensitivity ([[luminous efficiency]] function) `wavelength dependent`
    - $W=Js^{-1}=kg~m^2s^{-3}$
- $A=C~s^{-1}$ amount of charge flowing per second
    - $C\approx6.241509\times10^{18}$ elementary charges `like electrons, just constant counting unit for elementary charges`
