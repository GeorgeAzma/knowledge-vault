Most natural units possible `eliminates lots of conversion constants`
1. $c$ [[light|speed of light]]
2. $\hbar$ [[planck's constant|reduced planck's constant]]
3. $G$ [[gravity|gravitational constant]]
- $k_B$ [[boltzmann constant]]
##### $c=\hbar=G=k_B=1\approx1.05457181765\times 10^{-34}Js$
- $l_P=\sqrt{\dfrac{\hbar G}{c^3}}\approx1.616\times10^{-35}$ **planck length**
- $t_P=\sqrt{\dfrac{\hbar G}{c^5}}\approx 5.39\times 10^{-44}$ **planck time**
- $m_P=\sqrt{\dfrac{\hbar c}{G}}\approx 2.18\times 10^{-8}\text{ kg}$ **planck mass** `same as dust particle mass (surprisingly, gravity is weak)`
- $E_P=m_Pc^2\approx1.22\times10^{19}\text{ GeV}$ **planck energy**
