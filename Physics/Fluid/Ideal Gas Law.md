### Formula $PV=nRT$
- $P$ pressure
- $V$ volume
- $n$ amount of substance
- $R=8.31446261815324JK^{-1}\text{mol}^{-1}$ `ideal gas constant / molar gas constant` 
    - [[Boltzmann Constant]] but uses moles instead of exact molecule count `converts temperature -> energy units`
- $T$ [[temperature]]
### Rewrites
- $\rho=\dfrac{P}{RT}$ where $\rho$ is density $\dfrac{n}{V}$
