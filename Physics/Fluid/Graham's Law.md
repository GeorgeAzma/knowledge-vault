lighter gases spread faster than heavier gases at same [[temperature]]/pressure
`to preserve kinetic energy [1/2 mv^2]`
### $\dfrac{\text{Rate}_A}{\text{Rate}_B}=\sqrt{\dfrac{M_B}{M_A}}$
Diffusion/Effusion speed `effusion = diffusion through pinhole`
![[Graham's Law.webp#rounded]]
> [!example] helium $M\approx 4g/mol$, nitrogen $M\approx 28g/mol$
> - $\dfrac{\text{Rate}_{He}}{\text{Rate}_{N_2}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{28}{4}}=\sqrt{7}\approx 2.65$
> - hellium **effuses** `x2.65` faster than nitrogen
