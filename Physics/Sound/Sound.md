### Formulas
- **Frequency** $f=\dfrac{1}{t}$
- **Period** $t=\dfrac{1}{f}$
- **Total Vibrations** $v=ft$
- **Cycle Length** $\lambda$
- **Wave Speed** $s=f\lambda$ [[#Speed of Sound]]
- **Power/Amplitude** $P=(\Delta p)^2$  
	- $\Delta p=\text{Pressure Variation}$ 
	- Measured in [[Decibel|decibels]]
- **Intensity** $I=\dfrac{P^2}{2\rho s}$
	- According to [[Kinetic Energy|Kinetic Energy Formula]] $\dfrac{1}{2} mv^2$
	- $\rho$ Air [[Density]]
### Speed of Sound
Speed of Sound in dry air at $20\ C\degree$ is $343~m/s$
#### Formula $c=\sqrt{\dfrac{\text{stiffness}}{\text{density}}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{\gamma k_BT}{m}}$
$=\sqrt{\dfrac{\gamma RT}{M}}$ [[Ideal Gas Law]] `molar version`
- $\gamma$ adiabatic index ([[Degrees of Freedom|DoF]] dependent, $5/3$ monatomic `helium`, $7/5$ diatomic)
- $m$ particle mass
- $T$ [[temperature]]
- $v_{rms}=\sqrt{\dfrac{3k_BT}{m}}$ molecular thermal speed `root mean square of molecules in gas`
    - so $c/v_{rms}=\sqrt{\gamma/3}$ for monatomic $\approx 0.75$, for diatomic $\approx 0.68$
    - so sound speed is fixed fraction of molecular thermal speed
##### Particle Velocities
Follows [[Maxwell-Boltzmann Distribution]]
- $v_p=\sqrt{\dfrac{2k_BT}{m}}$ `most probable velocity (for a particle)`
- $\bar{v}=\sqrt{\dfrac{8k_BT}{\pi m}}$ `average velocity`
- $v_{rms}=\sqrt{\dfrac{3k_BT}{m}}$ `root mean square velocity`
