### Formula $H(q,p,t)=T+V=\sum_i p_i\dot{q_i}-L(q,\dot{q},t)$
- $H=T+V$ **Hamiltonian** = [[Kinetic Energy|Kinetic]] + [[Potential Energy|Potential]] Energy = **Total Energy**
- $p_i=\dfrac{\partial L}{\partial \dot{q}_i}=m\dot{x}$ conjugate momenta `momentum`
- $L(q,\dot{q},t)$ [[Lagrangian]]
### Hamilton's Equations $\dot{q_i}=\dfrac{\partial H}{\partial p_i},\quad\dot{p_i}=-\dfrac{\partial H}{\partial q_i}$
- $\dot{q_i}$ generalized velocities `position`


> [!example] [[Spring]] $m\ddot{x}=-kx$, finding using hamiltonian
> $L=T-V=\dfrac{1}{2}m\dot{x}^2-\dfrac{1}{2}kx^2$ `lagrangian`
> $p=\dfrac{\partial L}{\partial \dot{x}}=m\dot{x}$ `momentum`
> $H=p\dot{x}-L=\dfrac{p^2}{2m}+\dfrac{1}{2}kx^2$ `hamiltonian`
> $\dot{x}=\dfrac{\partial H}{\partial p}=\dfrac{p}{m}$
> $\dot{p}=-\dfrac{\partial H}{\partial x}=-kx$
> $\dot{p}=m\ddot{x}\implies m\ddot{x}=-kx$
> > [!note] 
> > [[Lagrangian]] gave one 2nd order differential equation
> > Hamiltonian gave two 1st order differential equations ($\dot{q_i},\dot{p_i}$)

> [!note] Hamiltonian is often total energy $H=T+V$
> - $T$ [[Kinetic Energy]]
> - $V$ [[Potential Energy]]
