### [[Ideal Gas Law|For Ideal Gas]] $\dfrac{1}{2}m\braket{v^2}=\dfrac{3}{2}k_BT$
- $\braket{\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2}$ average [[kinetic energy]]
- $k_B$ [[boltzmann constant]]
- $T$ [[temperature]]
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$ since in 3D there are 3 translational [[degrees of freedom]]
###### Derivation
- $KE=\dfrac{1}{2}mv_x^2+\dfrac{1}{2}mv_y^2+\dfrac{1}{2}mv_z^2$
- equipartition says each of those three terms average to $\dfrac{1}{2}k_BT$
    - $\braket{\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2}=\braket{\dfrac{1}{2}mv_x^2}=\braket{\dfrac{1}{2}mv_y^2}=\braket{\dfrac{1}{2}mv_z^2}=\dfrac{1}{2}k_BT$
    - $\braket{\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2}=\dfrac{3}{2}k_BT$ `add terms up`
