[[Taylor Series]] at $a=0$ `centered at 0`
### Formula $f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{\dfrac{f^{(n)}(0)}{n!}x^n}$
- $e=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{n!}x^n$ where $f^{(n)}(x)=e^x\implies f^{(n)}(0)=e^0=1$ for all $n$ 
- $\cos x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^n\dfrac{x^{2n}}{(2n)!}$
- $\sin x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^n\dfrac{x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}$
- $\arctan x=\sum_{n=0}^\infty(-1)^n\dfrac{x^{2n+1}}{2n+1}$ for $|x|\leq1$
