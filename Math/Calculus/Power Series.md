### $\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n(x-c)^n$
- $a_n$ any number or expression
- $c$ center
- only converges if $|x-c|<R$
- if $|x-c|=R$ convergence must manually be checked
### Examples
- [[Geometric Series]]
- $\large\sum_{n=0}^\infty(-1)^nx^n=\dfrac{1}{1+x}$
- $\large\sum_{n=0}^\infty(-1)^nnx^n=\dfrac{1}{(1+x)^2}$ `derived from above, by differentiating both sides`
