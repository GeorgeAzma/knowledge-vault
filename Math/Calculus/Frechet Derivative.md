[[Derivative]] = linear map that best approximates $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ at $x$, meaning $f(x+h)\approx f(x)+f'(x)\cdot h$
**Frechet Derivative** = linear map that best approximates $f:\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}^m$ at $\mathbf{x}$, meaning $f(\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{h})= f(\mathbf{x})+Df(\mathbf{x})\cdot\mathbf{h}+o(||\mathbf{h}||)$

[[Partial Derivative]] = applying this linear map to $\mathbf{h}=$ [[Standard Basis Vector|basis vector]] `outputs vector
[[Jacobian Matrix|Jacobian]] = applying this linear map to all $\mathbf{h}_i=$ [[Standard Basis Vector|basis vectors]] `outputs matrix`
### $D(f(\mathbf{x}))$
$f(\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{h})=f(\mathbf{x})+Df(x)\mathbf{h}+o(||\mathbf{h}||)$
