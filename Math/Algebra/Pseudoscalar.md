### $I=e_1e_2\dots e_n$
- $e_n$ [[Standard Basis Vector|basis vector]]
### $I^{-1}=e_ne_{n-1}\dots e_1$
#### [[Hodge Star Operator]] $\star A=AI^{-1}$
#### $IA=(-1)^{k(n-k)}AI$
- $A$ is a $k$-vector, like [[bivector]] or [[Bivector|trivector]] 
##### 3D 
- $I=e_xe_ye_z$ `pseudoscalar` 
- $I^{-1}=-I$ `works in 2D`
- $I^2=-1$
- $I\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{v}I$
- $\star\mathbf{v}=-I\mathbf{v}$ 
- $\star B=-IB$ `bivector`
- $BI=IB$ `bivector`
###### 3D Derivations
- $I^{-1}=-I$ since $I^{-1}=e_ze_ye_x=-e_xe_ye_z=-I$
- $IB=BI$ for [[bivector]] since 
    - $IA=(-1)^{2(3-2)}AI$
    - $IA=(-1)^2AI$
    - $IA=AI$
- $\star B=BI^{-1}=-BI=-IB$
