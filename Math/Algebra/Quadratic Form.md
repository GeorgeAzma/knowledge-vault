### [[Matrix]] $\mathbf{x}^TA\mathbf{x}=\sum_{i,j}x_iA_{ij}x_j$
- How much $A$ aligns with $\mathbf{x}$
- Imagine $x_i,x_j$ as nodes and $A_{ij}$ as their connection strength
     then $\mathbf{x}^TA\mathbf{x}$ measures how strongly connected the graph is
     $A$ is like different connection weight configurations
- $=\sum_iA_{ii}x_i^2+2\sum_{i<j}A_{ij}x_ix_j$ [[Symmetric Matrix]]
