### Properties
- $\dfrac{1}{\phi}=\phi-1$
- $\phi^2=\phi+1$
- $1+\dfrac{1}{\phi}=\phi$
- $\phi^2-\phi-1=0$

> [!note] Used in vogel's method, which distributes points almost evenly on a circle/sphere
### Metallic Ratio $\dfrac{na+b}{a}=\dfrac{a}{b}$ which is $\dfrac{n+\sqrt{n^2+4}}{2}=n+\dfrac{1}{n+\dfrac{1}{n+\dfrac{1}{\dots}}}$ 
- **Silver Ratio** $\dfrac{2a+b}{a}=\dfrac{a}{b}$ which is $1+\sqrt{2}$
