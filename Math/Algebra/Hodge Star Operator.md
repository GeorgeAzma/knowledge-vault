Used for converting [[bivector]][[s]] `which are like oriented planes`, to 3D [[vector]][[s]] perpendicular to it and vice-versa

but it is more general and can convert any $k$-dimensional oriented quantity 
into $(n-k)$-dimensional quantity in $n$-dimensional space
### Formula $\star A=AI^{-1}$
- $A$  [[Multivector]], like [[bivector]] `complex number`, [[Bivector|trivector]] `quaternion`...
- $I^{-1}=e_ze_ye_x$ [[pseudoscalar]]
**Why it works** $e_xI^{-1}=e_x(e_ze_ye_x)=-e_xe_xe_ze_y=e_ye_z$
##### 3D $\begin{array}{c}\star e_x=e_y\wedge e_z\\\star e_y=e_z\wedge e_x\\\star e_z=e_x\wedge e_y\end{array}$
