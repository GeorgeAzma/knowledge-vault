Repeatedly multiplying vector with a matrix aligns it to matrix's [[Eigenvector]] with largest [[Eigenvalue]]
- $v_1\approx\vec{x}_{n+1}=\dfrac{A\vec{x}_n}{||A\vec{x}_n||}$ where $\vec{x}\neq0$
- $\lambda_1\approx\dfrac{x_n^TAv_n}{x_n^Tx_n}$ `it's exact for symmetric matrices (like covariance matrix)`
