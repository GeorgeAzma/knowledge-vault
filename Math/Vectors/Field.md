Field attaches value to every point in space/domain
**Scalar Field** $f(x,y,z)\to\mathbb{R}$
[[Vector Field]] $\mathbf{F}(\mathbf{v})\to\mathbb{R}^n$
### $F:\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^n\to V$
- $\Omega$ [[Function|domain]]
- $V$ some value space
    - $\mathbb{R}$ for scalar field
    - $\mathbb{R}^n$ for [[vector field]]
