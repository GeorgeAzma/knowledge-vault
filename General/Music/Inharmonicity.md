`TL;DR` for pianos higher frequency harmonics are higher than expected `instead of being exact integer multiple`
### Explanation
Perfect strings have overtones/harmonics that are integer multiples of the fundamental frequency
`e.g. 440hz, 880hz, 1320hz...`
- but piano strings have stiffness 
- higher frequencies cause more local bending
- string stiffness tries to restore string to resting position
- this causes more force than expected for higher frequencies
- causing frequencies to be even higher than expected
### $f_n=nf_0\sqrt{1+Bn^2}$
`values fitted to Yamaha C5 piano`
- $f_0\approx 440.28$ fundamental
- $B\approx0.000637$ inharmonicty coefficient `or 0.0006355`
- $n$ integer multiple of fundamental note
